Intelligent Recruitment & Feature Match Engine

NLP Resume Screening & Candidate Ranking System

An enterprise-grade Natural Language Processing (NLP) system designed to evaluate candidate resumes against Job Descriptions through multi-feature match scoring, TF-IDF vector space modeling, and Cosine Similarity ranking.

Start Screening Resumes Run Sample Demo

spaCy NLP Preprocessing

Lowers casing, tokenizes, removes stopwords/punctuation, and lemmatizes while safely preserving technical terms like C++, C#, .NET, Node.js, and B.Tech.

Multi-Feature Match Engine

Extracts skills against a comprehensive taxonomy, evaluates candidate experience in years, and maps degree equivalencies (B.Tech, B.E., MCA, M.Tech).

TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity

Transforms job descriptions and candidate resumes into TF-IDF vector spaces and computes cosine similarity matrices for textual relevance scoring.

Candidate Scoring Engine Formula

Candidates are objectively ranked from highest overall score to lowest using configured weightings:

Final Score = (0.40 × Skill Match) + (0.25 × TF-IDF Similarity) + (0.20 × Experience Match) + (0.15 × Education Match)
